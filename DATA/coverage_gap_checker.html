Coverage Gap Checker

Enter numbers from your declarations page to see where your car or home coverage may differ from state rules or common gaps. Covers California, Texas, Florida and New York (auto) and California, Texas and Florida (home).

Educational tool, not insurance advice, not a quote, and not a coverage decision. It compares what you type with rules we checked on and cannot see your actual policy. Your insurer's policy language controls. Nothing you enter leaves your device; nothing is saved. Confirm anything important with your insurer or a licensed professional.
